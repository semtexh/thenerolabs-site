Privacy Policy

The Nero Labs LLC · Last updated: June 4, 2026

The Nero Labs LLC (“The Nero Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) builds professional mobile applications for demanding environments — including MapNero, BallisticEdge, CarCheckUp, and StreamEar (collectively, the “Apps”). This Privacy Policy explains what information our Apps and website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to keeping data collection to the minimum needed to make our products work.

The short version. Most of our Apps work on your device and do not require an account. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising. Where an App needs a permission (such as location or camera), it is used only to deliver the feature you requested.

1.Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on which App or service you use.

1.1 Information you provide

  • Support communications. If you email support@thenerolabs.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.
  • In-app input. Data you enter to use a feature — for example map markers and overlays in MapNero, ballistic parameters in BallisticEdge, vehicle checklists in CarCheckUp, or alert preferences in StreamEar. This data is generally stored locally on your device unless a feature explicitly syncs it.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & diagnostic data. If you opt in to share diagnostics, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash logs and performance data to help us fix bugs.
  • Approximate usage analytics. Where enabled, we may collect aggregated, non-identifying usage metrics (such as which features are opened) to improve our products. These metrics are not linked to your identity.

1.3 Permissions some Apps may request

PermissionWhy it is used
LocationTo show your position on the map and provide location-relevant features (MapNero, StreamEar regional alerts). Used only while you use the feature.
Camera / PhotosTo capture or import images for overlays, checklists, or reports (MapNero, CarCheckUp).
NotificationsTo deliver alerts and reminders you have chosen to receive (StreamEar, CarCheckUp).
Local storageTo save your work and settings on your device.

You can review and change these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings.

2.How We Use Information

We use information solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the features of our Apps and website;
  • Diagnose and fix crashes, bugs, and performance issues;
  • Understand, in aggregate, how features are used so we can improve them;
  • Respond to your support requests; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3.What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not require an account or collect more data than a feature needs.

4.Data Storage & Security

Most data created in our Apps is stored locally on your device. Where a feature transmits data (for example, fetching alert feeds in StreamEar), it is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5.Third-Party Services

Our Apps may rely on the following categories of third-party services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple — App Store distribution, push notifications (APNs), and optional crash/diagnostic reporting.
  • Mapping & data providers — map tiles and public data feeds used to power location and alert features.
  • Infrastructure providers — content delivery and notification infrastructure (for example, Cloudflare) used to serve real-time features.

6.Children’s Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7.Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because most data stays on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly by editing or deleting data in the App or uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8.International Users

The Nero Labs LLC is based in Wyoming, USA. If you access our Apps or website from outside the United States, any information that is transmitted may be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted within the relevant App or on this website where appropriate.
