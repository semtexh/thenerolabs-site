Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

The Nero Labs LLC ("The Nero Labs", "we", "us", or "our") builds professional mobile applications for demanding environments — including MapNero, BallisticEdge, CarCheckUp, and StreamEar (the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains what our Apps and website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to make our products work.

The short version. Most of our Apps work on your device and need no account. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your data for advertising. Where an App needs a permission (such as location or camera), it is used only to deliver the feature you requested.

1. Information We Collect

What we collect depends on which App or service you use.

Information you provide: if you email support@thenerolabs.com we receive your email address and message. Data you enter to use a feature — map markers in MapNero, ballistic parameters in BallisticEdge, vehicle checklists in CarCheckUp, alert preferences in StreamEar — is generally stored locally on your device unless a feature explicitly syncs it.

Information collected automatically: if you opt in, Apple may share anonymized crash and performance data; where enabled, we may collect aggregated, non-identifying usage metrics to improve our products.

2. Permissions Some Apps May Request

  • Location — to show your position and provide location-relevant features (MapNero, StreamEar). Used only while you use the feature.
  • Camera / Photos — to capture or import images for overlays, checklists, or reports.
  • Notifications — to deliver alerts and reminders you chose to receive.
  • Local storage — to save your work and settings on your device.
You can review and change these permissions at any time in your device Settings.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to provide, operate, and maintain our Apps and website; diagnose and fix crashes and bugs; understand in aggregate how features are used; respond to your support requests; and comply with legal obligations.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not require an account or collect more data than a feature needs.

5. Data Storage & Security

Most data created in our Apps is stored locally on your device. Where a feature transmits data (for example, alert feeds in StreamEar), it is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

Our Apps may rely on third-party services governed by their own privacy policies: Apple (App Store distribution, push notifications, optional diagnostics); mapping and data providers (map tiles and public data feeds); and infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare (content delivery and notifications).

7. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA — including access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection. Because most data stays on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly by editing or deleting data in the App or uninstalling it. For anything else, contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. International Users

The Nero Labs LLC is based in Wyoming, USA. If you access our Apps or website from outside the United States, any transmitted information may be processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.
