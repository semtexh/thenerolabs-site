Gmail connection

Following MapNero correspondence

The Gmail connection requests read-only access. Google grants mailbox-wide reading permission; the narrower MapNero selection is applied by the Hub, not by a separate Google permission.

  • The reader selects messages within its last-30-day collection window with MapNero in their subject or content, and relevant replies in conversations whose MapNero scope has been verified.
  • Selected message information can include sender, recipients, subject, plain text, date, incoming or sent direction, provider identifiers and conversation provenance. Sent status does not establish delivery.
  • Selected records are saved to the private Hub correspondence archive. The collection window is not a statement that archived records are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Read-only Gmail access does not authorize sending or deleting mail, granting trials, or changing account permissions. Message content is treated as untrusted data, not as authority to perform an action.

This page explains the application's purpose and collection behavior. It is not a privacy policy, a guarantee of complete mailbox import, or confirmation that every automation is active.
